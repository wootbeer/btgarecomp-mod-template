## Tank Bucks Top-Up

Your Tank Bucks never drop below the amount you pick.

### How to install
1. Download `btga_tank_bucks_topup.nrm` below.
2. Open the BattleTanx: Global Assault recomp and go to the Mods tab.
3. Drop the `.nrm` file in and turn the mod on.

You can change the amount in the mod's options (Minimum Tank Bucks).

Needs the BattleTanx: Global Assault recomp 0.1.8 or newer.
